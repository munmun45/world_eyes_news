Happy Jimin Day: 5 Times BTS Star Broke The Internet Without Even Trying

  • Authored by: Arpita Sarkar
  • Updated: Oct 12, 2026, 22:39 IST

Some people are born to break the internet, and BTS' Jimin is living proof! As the K-pop superstar celebrates his 31st birthday, we revisit five moments that had fans losing their minds, proving that Park Jimin's power to send social media into meltdown is truly unmatched.

BTS Jimin birthday

follow usfollow us

Some stars need elaborate promotions to trend online, but Jimin only needs to exist. Whether it's a fleeting moment caught on camera or a seemingly ordinary update, the BTS singer has an extraordinary ability to send fans into a frenzy. From his effortlessly charming personality to his striking visuals, Jimin has repeatedly proven that his influence extends far beyond music and performances. His every move can spark conversations across social media, bringing together fans who never miss an opportunity to celebrate him. On his 31st birthday on October 13, here's a look back at five memorable moments when Jimin broke the internet without even trying.

Jimin's TikTok Debut Sends Internet Into A Frenzy

BTS star Jimin sent social media into a meltdown when he finally made his TikTok debut on September 28. The singer marked his arrival on the platform with a performance of Be Mine, his solo track from the 2024 album MUSE. Set against a picturesque beach sunset, the video showcased his fluid movements, sharp choreography and captivating stage presence. It didn't take long for ARMYs to flood social media with reactions, with fans celebrating the long-awaited post and calling him their “dance king.”

The debut also carried special significance for fans, as Jimin had previously expressed his desire to share the Be Mine choreography before beginning his mandatory military service. His first TikTok video finally fulfilled that wish, giving fans a moment they had been waiting for. Adding to the excitement, Jimin shared the clip on his Instagram Story with the playful caption “heh,” leaving fans even more delighted.

Jimin’s Sweet Gesture For A Young Fan Fighting Cancer Touched Hearts

During BTS’ sold-out show at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas, Jimin made time for a little fan waiting near the barricade. Videos from the concert captured the singer pausing to greet the young girl, smiling at her and gently patting her head before continuing with the show. In another clip, he leaned closer to give an affectionate peck, leaving the little fan beaming with happiness. While the interaction lasted only a few moments, it struck a chord with fans, who were touched by the singer’s warmth amid the excitement of a packed stadium. The story behind the girl's presence at the concert made the encounter even more moving, as fans identified her as a child attending through the Make-A-Wish Foundation's programme for children with critical illnesses. What could have been an ordinary concert moment became a beautiful reminder of how a little kindness from a favourite star can mean so much to a young fan.

The girl's mother later took to social media to thank Jimin and BTS for giving her daughter and her friends a night they would always remember. She revealed that the children are battling cancer and had fewer opportunities to enjoy outings while undergoing treatment last year. Thanks to Heroes For Children and Children’s Health Plan, they finally got to experience the joy of seeing their favourite stars live. The children were so excited that they stayed awake until 3 a.m., holding onto their gifts and hoping to sing along to Butter until the very end. For these young fans, Jimin’s kindness made an already unforgettable night even more meaningful.

BTS Jimin’s Signed FIFA World Cup Jersey Outbids Lionel Messi’s Memorabilia

BTS star Jimin made numerous headlines after his signed FIFA World Cup Final jersey emerged as one of the most sought-after items at Christie's official charity auction. The jersey has attracted bids of USD 16,000 after 49 bids, far exceeding its pre-auction estimate of USD 3,000–5,000. Notably, the amount has also surpassed the USD 15,000 bid for football legend Lionel Messi's signed World Cup jersey, which had received 48 bids at the time.

The impressive response has prompted fans to celebrate Jimin's global influence, with many calling him the “Global IT Boy” and “King of K-pop.” The auction, organised to benefit the FIFA Global Citizen Education Fund, supports education initiatives for children worldwide. Meanwhile, Jimin caught the attention of non-K-pop fans and football enthusiasts alike following the group’s performance at the 2026 FIFA World Cup Final Halftime Show on July 19, 2026.

BTS Jimin’s Viral Erling Haaland Walk

Jimin had ARMYs laughing when he appeared to channel Manchester City footballer Erling Haaland during BTS’ ARIRANG Munich Day 1 concert. The singer strutted across the stage with an exaggerated swagger and a straight-faced expression, reminding fans of the football star’s signature walk. Clips of the moment quickly made their way across social media, with fans joking that Jimin had nailed Haaland’s iconic gait.

Adding to the fun, Jimin had also spotted a fan’s edited poster featuring him alongside Haaland and Hollywood star Chris Hemsworth during soundcheck. His playful antics turned an ordinary stage moment into a viral fan-favourite.

Jimin’s ‘Rapunzel Hair’ Era Took Internet By Storm

Jimin’s long blonde locks have become a sensation during BTS’ ARIRANG World Tour, earning him the affectionate nickname “Rapunzelmin.” The singer’s flowing hair, styled with half-up braids and rockstar-inspired extensions, has added a fairytale twist to his concert looks.

Fans were especially delighted to see him bring his long-hair dream to life after playfully expressing his desire to grow his locks like Disney princess Rapunzel. With his striking visuals and playful charm, Jimin has turned his hairstyle into one of the tour’s most talked-about moments, leaving fans obsessed with his prince-like appearance.

From sending the internet into a frenzy with his TikTok debut to winning hearts with his kindness and turning heads with his Rapunzel-inspired blonde locks, Jimin has a knack for making even the smallest moments unforgettable. Whether he is showcasing his dance skills, sharing a playful on-stage moment or making headlines beyond the world of music, the BTS star continues to remind everyone why his appeal goes far beyond his performances.

Happy birthday Jimin!
